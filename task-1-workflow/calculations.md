# Illustrative net time-saved calculation

**All numbers below are assumptions for demonstrating the method, not measured IFHE data.** Replace them if an authorized, privacy-preserving measurement becomes available.

## Assumptions
- 100 requests per month (illustrative volume).
- Current average handling effort: 12 minutes per request.
- Proposed average handling effort before review/support: 7 minutes per request.
- Additional review, monitoring and support effort: 2 minutes per request.

## Calculation
- Current effort = 100 × 12 = 1,200 minutes/month.
- Proposed gross effort = 100 × 7 = 700 minutes/month.
- Added review/support effort = 100 × 2 = 200 minutes/month.
- Proposed total effort = 700 + 200 = 900 minutes/month.
- **Net time saved = 1,200 − 900 = 300 minutes/month = 5 hours/month.**
- Net saving per request = 12 − 7 − 2 = 3 minutes/request.
- Relative net saving = 300 / 1,200 × 100 = **25%**.

## Sensitivity
The result depends on actual volume and actual time per case. If only 20 requests/month occur, the same assumed net saving is 60 minutes/month. If support effort exceeds the five-minute gross saving, net savings become zero or negative.

## How to measure honestly
For an approved pilot, record anonymous aggregate counts and time samples only; define start/end points; include incomplete cases and rework; report median and range, not just a single average; and compare similar periods. Do not collect student names, IDs, marks, fees, credentials or private messages.
