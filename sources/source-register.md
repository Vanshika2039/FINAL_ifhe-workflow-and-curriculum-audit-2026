## Source Verification Rules

1. Record the actual access date for each source.
2. Distinguish publication dates from access dates.
3. Prefer official institutional documents for curriculum and regulatory claims.
4. Use at least three genuine job descriptions with verifiable dates for the curriculum comparison.
5. If a job description does not display a publication date, explicitly state that the date is unavailable.
6. Link each important factual claim in the reports to its supporting source.
7. Label assumptions, estimates, simulated scenarios, and unverified details.
8. Do not publish confidential institutional documents or identifiable student information.
