# Limitations and Scope

**Project:** College Workflow and CSE Curriculum Audit  
**Institution:** ICFAI Tech School, IFHE Hyderabad  
**Admission batch:** 2023–2027  
**Last updated:** 24 October 2026

## 1. Workflow Investigation

- I do not currently have authorised access to observe an actual college administrative workflow.
- Therefore, any workflow documented before obtaining permission and collecting evidence must be labelled as a hypothetical simulation or proposed process redesign.
- Estimated request volumes, processing times, delays, and time savings are assumptions for scenario analysis, not measured institutional results.
- Synthetic test cases are used to test the proposed design without accessing real student or faculty records.
- No real employee, faculty member, or student has been represented as providing feedback unless their feedback is actually obtained with permission.

## 2. Curriculum Verification

- The working course list is based on student-provided information for the B.Tech CSE 2023–2027 batch.
- The applicable official syllabus and academic regulations have not yet been verified against the complete working list.
- The public programme pages are general references and must not be treated as definitive evidence for every course, credit, prerequisite, or regulation applicable to this admission batch.
- Semester-wise credits, total programme credits, elective rules, internship requirements, and prerequisites must be verified before final calculations or proposed credit changes are presented as compliant.
- The Semester 5 course-code mapping supplied for this working audit is CS302 — Operating Systems and CS303 — Programming Language and Compiler Construction. It remains subject to official-document verification.

## 3. Job-Market Comparison

- At least three genuine job descriptions must be checked before the curriculum comparison is considered complete.
- Employer, role title, required skills, publication date, and URL should be recorded from the original job listing wherever available.
- Job descriptions reflect individual roles and do not represent the requirements of every employer.
- Missing publication dates must be reported as unavailable rather than estimated.

## 4. AI-Assisted Work

- AI-generated suggestions are treated as drafts, not authoritative evidence.
- Important factual claims, code, calculations, course recommendations, and regulatory statements must be independently checked.
- AI prompts, model or tool used, date, relevant output, and verification results should be recorded.
- Personal contribution should distinguish work completed independently, work assisted by AI, and work that remains incomplete.

## 5. Privacy and Ethics

- No confidential institutional documents or identifiable student records should be published.
- Workflow testing should use synthetic data.
- Any real workflow observation or feedback must be collected only with appropriate permission.
- Unavailable evidence and incomplete checks will be disclosed in the final report.

## 6. Current Status

This repository is a working draft. It must not be described as a completed, officially validated audit until the outstanding source checks, calculations, comparisons, and tests have been completed.
